Programmer options

Each section is a separate choice, so codes combine, like A2 B2 C2. Every frame is the real page at 1440x900; click one to open it full size.

A. Layout: where the spare height goes

A1 Current (current)Scene changes shares its column with the empty end of Palettes, so only about 6 rows show.
A2 Centre columnScene pane runs full height, so Scene changes gets most of the page. Palettes sits under colour and takes what's left.
A3 RackFaders, wheel and pad line up in one row at eye level. Palettes and a wider Scene pane share the bottom. Lists get narrower.

B. Page bar: the row of identical buttons

B1 Current (current)Everything in one row, with Blackout in the middle.
B2 GroupedSame buttons, grouped into selection and scene. Blackout moves to the far right, where it's always in the same place.
B3 Moved into panesLimit and Clear move to Fixtures, and Add moves to Animations. The bar keeps only Blackout and Exclusive preview. Palettes (0) and Animations (0) go, since those panes show their own counts.

C. Fader bank: size and spacing

C1 Current (current)368 x 320. Compact, but the Intensity and Strobe labels nearly touch.
C2 Wider440 x 320. Clean labels and easier to grab, but lists lose 72px.
C3 Wider and taller440 x 420. Finer control on a mouse, and uses more of the page. Pushes colour lower.
C4 Narrow caps368 x 320 with 28px caps and smaller labels. Keeps list width, but the caps are a smaller target.
