1 Blackout is global and always visible

Was: invisible on most pages, lost when changing page, strobed when held, blocked by text fields. Now: the status bar owns it on every page. Cmd/Ctrl+B works even while typing, ignores key repeat, and restores your level.

Grand master

Press Esc while dragging to cancel and return to the starting value. Reset now sits away from Blackout and has a clear name.

2 One honest connection state

Was: disconnected still showed Saved, BPM and moving graphs, a banner hid the top row, and Blackout claimed success. Now: use the buttons at the top. The strip pushes content down instead of covering it, live values freeze and dim, and commands say they weren't sent.

Last known level

While disconnected, try Blackout or Save. Commands aren't queued, because replaying lighting actions later could be dangerous.

3 One save state, with real undo

Was: "Saved" stayed on after edits and failed saves, while Load and Close always claimed unsaved changes. Undo all wiped 316 changes for good. Now: status bar and dialogs read one state, and clearing changes can be undone with Cmd/Ctrl+Z.

Scene changes
4 Dialogs are properly modal

Was: Tab escaped into the page, a second dialog could open on top, and clicking text broke Esc. Now: focus stays inside, the page behind is locked, and Cancel is the default. Blackout is the one exception: it still works, and the dialog confirms it.

5 Add fixture has no dead ends

Was: no group field, defaulted to the offline universe, accepted channel 99999, and the new fixture never appeared. Now: group is chosen up front, defaults avoid offline outputs, bad values block Add, and the row appears selected.
