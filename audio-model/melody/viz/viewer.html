Club lights

This is the Club look; the Look menu under the club has the others. Pacing follows the Rekordbox beat grid; the key sets the palette (circle of fifths), the sounding vocal / lead note the accent colour, melody activity the pattern speed, phrases the ceiling pattern. Lights pulse on the real drum hits, dim with the loudness and change pattern and colour whenever the music changes. Only the drop strobes: from the moment the bass hits, for up to one bar. Builds run the lights towards it and black out on the last half beat.
